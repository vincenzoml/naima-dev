# Naima ships the rule that long work reports its progress, or says why it cannot and what it reports instead

Specification: specs/progress-long-work-says-how-far, section 1.

Checked by: `naima rules --audience agents` (and `--audience people`) lists the rule long-work/long-work-reports-progress as MUST, for everyone, enforced by progress-declared, with its acknowledgement "Progress mode on"; a project can retire it.

Why: the owner's rule of 2026-10-10 — every long-running operation reports progress unless that is really impossible; a pbessolve ran 11 hours with nothing to show it was moving (specification §0).
